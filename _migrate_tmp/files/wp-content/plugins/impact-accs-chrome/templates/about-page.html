ORIGINS

Our team spent years in account supply and media buying before building the market. We saw how much launch speed depends on access — teams move fast when they have the right resource. But we also witnessed firsthand the challenges and complexity. The chat hunting. The bad supply. The launches delayed by unreliable sellers.

SPARK

Over five years in the market, we built a reliable supply channel for teams running traffic at scale. But account supply still felt painfully chaotic, stuck in Telegram noise. That's when it clicked: teams don't need another chat — they need a structured access channel.

MISSION

To become the default access channel for media buying teams that need speed and stability. Structured access infrastructure — clear, fast, and built for teams who launch.

VISION

A world where teams launch without hunting for access — clear terms, fast contact, repeat orders. Where teams spend their time scaling campaigns, not firefighting in random chats.
